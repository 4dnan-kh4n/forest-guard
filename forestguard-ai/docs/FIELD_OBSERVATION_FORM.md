Attach the original photos and patch map/sketch/geometry. Distinguish camera
location from the observed patch. Keep acquisition dates, location uncertainty
and estimated measurements explicit. For an existing historical record, also
include its original reference date, source ID and whether the observation was
directly recorded or inferred. Leave train/validation/test grouping unassigned;
we arrange it before model sampling and keep independent test evidence reserved.
